# Style rules for the Balkan language (`sh`)

One text for Bosnia, Croatia, Montenegro and Serbia: Latin script, ijekavian, descriptive everyday words that all four countries use and understand, no national marker words. Politics stays out of the wording.

Rules (decided by the owner):
- Prefer the word that is the same everywhere, or an everyday descriptive phrase, over a word that sounds Croatian, Serbian or Bosnian. Never use two spellings of one thing in the same text.
- **Files and uploads:** "fajl" (not datoteka), "dodati / dodajem / dodavanje" for upload ("Posjedujem prava na fajlove koje dodajem na sistem"), not "otpremiti / otpremam" (Croatian only), not "učitati" for uploading. "Učitavanje" stays for a page or resource that loads. If a term needs an explanation or has no common word, write it with its alternative once, for example "dodavanje (otpremanje)", and keep that form every time.
- **System:** "sistem" (not sustav). **Server:** "server". **Account:** "nalog" as now unless the owner says otherwise.
- ijekavian forms: vrijeme, mjesto, dijete, posjedujem, smijem, htjeti.
- Do not use "šaljem / šaljem" style national markers where a descriptive verb works ("dodajem", "pišem", "dajem").
- Numbers and placeholders as in the other languages (`{{policy.age_gate}}` is a number; never decline a placeholder).
