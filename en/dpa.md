# Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

*Version {{doc.version}}, last updated {{doc.updated}}.*

This DPA is part of the agreement (the [Terms of Service]({{url.terms}}) or a signed order) between **{{company.name}}**, {{company.address}} ("**Processor**", "we") and the customer ("**Controller**", "you") for the Services. It applies when we process personal data on your behalf. It forms an agreement on acceptance of the Terms; no separate signature is needed. Where a signed copy is required, request it at [{{company.legal_email}}](mailto:{{company.legal_email}}).

## 1. Definitions {#definitions}

"**Data Protection Law**" means all law applicable to the processing, including Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (**GDPR**), the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, the Swiss FADP, the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection (**ZZPL**), and US state privacy laws such as the CCPA/CPRA. "**Personal Data**", "**Processing**", "**Controller**", "**Processor**", "**Data Subject**", "**Supervisory Authority**" and "**Personal Data Breach**" have the meanings in that law. "**Customer Personal Data**" is Personal Data in Your Content that we process for you. "**Sub-processor**" is a third party we engage to process Customer Personal Data. "**SCCs**" are the Standard Contractual Clauses annexed to Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914. "**Restricted Transfer**" is a transfer that requires a transfer mechanism under Data Protection Law.

## 2. Roles and scope {#roles}

2.1 For Customer Personal Data you are the Controller (or a Processor acting for another Controller, in which case we are a sub-processor and you guarantee you are authorised to give these instructions) and we are the Processor.

2.2 We act as an independent Controller for our own purposes: account administration, billing, security, abuse prevention, service improvement and legal compliance, as described in the [Privacy Policy]({{url.privacy}}).

2.3 Annex I describes the processing: subject-matter, duration, nature, purpose, data types and Data Subjects.

## 3. Our obligations {#obligations}

We will:

3.1 process Customer Personal Data **only on your documented instructions**, which are the Terms, this DPA, your configuration and use of the Services, and further reasonable written instructions consistent with them. We tell you promptly if we think an instruction breaches Data Protection Law (and may suspend that processing until clarified);

3.2 not process it for other purposes (including our own marketing or model training), not sell or "share" it for cross-context advertising, not retain, use or disclose it outside the direct business relationship, and not combine it with other data, except as the law allows or requires;

3.3 ensure persons authorised to process it are bound by confidentiality;

3.4 implement the **technical and organisational measures** in Annex II, and keep them appropriate to the risk;

3.5 comply with section 6 (Sub-processors), section 7 (transfers) and applicable law, and assist you as set out below;

3.6 notify you if applicable law requires us to process it otherwise, unless that law prohibits notification.

## 4. Your obligations {#controller-obligations}

You will: have a lawful basis and provide all required notices and consents for the processing; give lawful instructions; not submit special-category, children's, payment-card or other sensitive data unless the Services allow it for that purpose and you have assessed the risks; keep your accounts and credentials secure; and respond to Data Subjects and authorities as Controller.

## 5. Assistance, rights and audits {#assistance}

5.1 **Data Subject requests.** If a Data Subject contacts us about Customer Personal Data we redirect them to you unless the law prevents it, and give you reasonable help (usually through the Services' export, correction and deletion tools) at your cost where it goes beyond ordinary functionality.

5.2 **Security, DPIAs, consultations.** We reasonably help with security obligations, impact assessments and prior consultations, taking into account the nature of processing and the information we have.

5.3 **Breach.** We notify you **without undue delay and within {{policy.breach_hours}} hours** after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data, with the information we have (nature, categories and approximate numbers, likely consequences, measures taken) and update it as we learn more. We take steps to contain and remediate. A notice is not an admission of fault.

5.4 **Information and audit.** On request we provide information needed to show compliance with this DPA, such as current certifications, audit summaries or a completed security questionnaire. If that does not satisfy a legal requirement, you may audit us (or a bound-by-confidentiality independent auditor may), once per year (and after a breach or as a regulator requires), on 30 days' notice, in business hours, without disrupting operations, at your cost and subject to confidentiality and our security rules. Audits of our Sub-processors follow their audit terms.

## 6. Sub-processors {#subprocessors}

6.1 You give **general authorisation** to engage Sub-processors. The current list is at [{{url.subprocessors}}]({{url.subprocessors}}).

6.2 We give at least **{{policy.notice_days}} days' notice** of a new or replacement Sub-processor by updating that list and, if you have subscribed, emailing you.

6.3 You may object on reasonable data-protection grounds within that period. We then work with you in good faith to resolve it (including by not using the Sub-processor for you). If we cannot, either of us may terminate the affected Services, and we refund prepaid fees for the unused period.

6.4 We impose on each Sub-processor data-protection obligations no less protective than this DPA, and remain responsible for their performance.

## 7. International transfers {#transfers}

7.1 We may process Customer Personal Data in {{company.country}}{{#if hosting.region}}, {{hosting.region}}{{/if}} and in the countries of our Sub-processors.

7.2 For Restricted Transfers we rely on, in this order: an adequacy decision (including the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for certified recipients); otherwise the **SCCs**, which are incorporated by reference and completed as follows:
- **Module Two** (controller to processor) applies between you and us; **Module Three** (processor to processor) applies where you are a processor;
- clause 7 (docking) applies; clause 9(a): Option 2, general authorisation, notice period per section 6.2; clause 11(a): optional language does not apply; clause 17: Option 1, the law of the EU Member State of the exporter (Ireland, if none applies); clause 18(b): the courts of that Member State;
- Annex I.A (parties), I.B (description) and I.C (competent authority) are as in Annex I and the section on the supervisory authority below; Annex II is Annex II of this DPA; Annex III is the Sub-processor list.

7.3 For transfers subject to the **UK GDPR**, the ICO's **International Data Transfer Addendum** to the SCCs applies, with the Tables completed by this DPA. For transfers subject to the **Swiss FADP**, the SCCs apply with references to the GDPR read as the FADP, the Swiss FDPIC as competent authority, and "Member State" including Switzerland.

7.4 For transfers subject to **Serbian ZZPL**, the parties rely on standard contractual clauses or other mechanisms the ZZPL recognises, which this DPA is intended to satisfy; the Serbian Commissioner (Poverenik) is the competent authority.

7.5 If a transfer mechanism is invalidated or ceases to be available, the parties will work in good faith to put a valid replacement in place, and we may suspend the transfer meanwhile.

7.6 We will assess and tell you if we are legally unable to comply with the SCCs, and will challenge, to the extent reasonable, disclosure requests from public authorities that appear unlawful.

## 8. Deletion and return {#deletion}

On termination, and at your written request during the term, we **return or delete** Customer Personal Data (export through the Services is available for {{policy.retention_account_days}} days after termination). After that we delete it and existing copies, including from backups within 35 days, unless the law requires us to keep it, in which case we keep it confidential and process it only for that purpose. We confirm deletion on request.

## 9. US state privacy law {#us}

Where CCPA/CPRA or another US state privacy law applies, we are your "service provider" or "processor" and: (a) we process Customer Personal Data only for the business purposes in the agreement; (b) we do not sell or share it; (c) we do not retain, use or disclose it outside the direct business relationship or for any commercial purpose other than the specified business purposes; (d) we do not combine it with other data except as permitted; (e) we notify you if we can no longer meet our obligations; (f) you may take reasonable steps to ensure we use it consistently with your obligations and stop and remediate unauthorised use; and (g) we certify that we understand and will comply with these restrictions.

## 10. Liability and precedence {#liability}

10.1 The liability of each party under this DPA, including SCC liability to the extent permitted, is subject to the limits in the Terms. Nothing limits a Data Subject's rights under the SCCs or Data Protection Law, or either party's liability that cannot be limited.

10.2 If this DPA conflicts with the Terms on data protection, this DPA prevails; if it conflicts with the SCCs, the SCCs prevail.

## 11. Term and governing law {#term}

This DPA lasts as long as we process Customer Personal Data. It is governed by the law that governs the Terms ({{law.governing}}), except that the SCCs are governed as stated in section 7. Disputes are resolved as set out in the Terms.

## Annex II: Technical and organisational measures {#annex-2}

We maintain at least the following, and may update them if the level of protection is not materially reduced.

- **Governance:** information-security responsibility assigned; documented policies; staff confidentiality undertakings and security awareness training; vendor due diligence for Sub-processors.
- **Access control:** unique accounts, least privilege and role-based access; multi-factor authentication for administrative and production access; timely revocation on role change or leaving; periodic access reviews.
- **Encryption:** TLS for data in transit; encryption at rest for databases, object storage and backups; keys managed separately from data; hashed and salted password storage using a modern algorithm.
- **Network and system security:** segmentation, firewalls and default-deny rules; hardened, patched systems; vulnerability scanning and timely remediation; protection against malware and abuse; secure development practices with code review and dependency monitoring.
- **Logging and monitoring:** security-relevant logging with restricted access and defined retention; alerting for anomalous activity.
- **Availability and resilience:** redundant infrastructure where appropriate, regular tested backups, documented disaster-recovery and business-continuity plans.
- **Data separation and minimisation:** logical separation of customer data; test data not drawn from production without protection; collection limited to what is needed.
- **Incident response:** documented procedure for detection, triage, containment, notification (section 5.3) and post-incident review.
- **Physical security:** data processed in facilities of reputable infrastructure providers with physical access controls; offices and devices protected (screen locks, disk encryption, remote wipe).
- **Deletion:** secure deletion or anonymisation at end of retention, and secure disposal of media.
- **Assurance:** regular review of these measures; infrastructure providers hold recognised certifications (for example ISO/IEC 27001, SOC 2) where available.
