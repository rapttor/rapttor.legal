# Acceptable Use Policy

*Version {{doc.version}}, last updated {{doc.updated}}.*

This policy is part of the [Terms of Service]({{url.terms}}). It applies to everyone who uses {{product.name}} and the other Services of {{company.name}}, including your users, End Users and anyone acting with your credentials or keys. **You are responsible for all use of your account and for ensuring others you allow in follow this policy.** The examples below illustrate and do not limit; if something is harmful or unlawful, it is not allowed even if not listed.

## 1. Legal and safety basics {#legal}

You must not use the Services to:

1. violate any law or regulation, or help someone else do so, including consumer, advertising, data-protection, export, sanctions, anti-money-laundering, gambling and intellectual-property law;
2. create, upload, store, share or link to **child sexual abuse or exploitation material** or content that sexualises minors in any form. This is zero-tolerance: we remove it, close the account, preserve evidence and report to the competent authorities and hotlines without notice;
3. promote, threaten, plan or celebrate violence, terrorism, violent extremism, self-harm or eating disorders, or incite hatred or discrimination against people based on protected characteristics;
4. harass, stalk, bully, threaten, intimidate, dox or expose private information of any person, or share intimate images of someone without their consent (including synthetic or "deepfake" ones);
5. defraud, deceive or mislead people, including scams, phishing, pyramid or Ponzi schemes, fake reviews and testimonials, fake stores, impersonation, counterfeit goods, or fraudulent use of payment instruments;
6. sell, offer or facilitate illegal goods or services, including illegal drugs, weapons and explosives, stolen data or credentials, forged documents, human trafficking, or unlicensed regulated services;
7. operate unlicensed gambling, lotteries or sweepstakes, or any activity that requires a licence you do not hold;
8. infringe or misappropriate anyone's copyright, trademark, trade secret, privacy, publicity or other rights, or distribute pirated or cracked software or media.

## 2. Security and system abuse {#security}

You must not:

1. introduce or distribute malware, ransomware, spyware, cryptominers, botnet controllers or any harmful code;
2. attempt to gain unauthorised access to the Services, other accounts, our systems, other customers' data or networks connected to them, or probe, scan or test their vulnerabilities **without our prior written permission** (good-faith research under our [Security & Vulnerability Disclosure]({{url.security}}) policy is the only exception);
3. launch or assist denial-of-service, credential-stuffing, brute-force, enumeration or other attacks, or use the Services as a platform to attack third parties;
4. bypass, disable or interfere with authentication, rate limits, quotas, metering, geo-restrictions, anti-abuse, content-filtering or security features;
5. intercept, sniff or tamper with traffic or protocols, or run unauthorised proxies, emulators or modified clients;
6. overload or degrade the Services or other users' experience, including excessive automated requests, and any use that imposes a disproportionate load on our infrastructure (we may define "fair use" limits for any plan and enforce them);
7. use the Services to send **spam**, bulk unsolicited messages, deceptive or unlawfully targeted marketing, or to harvest email addresses or personal data; messages sent through the Services must comply with anti-spam and e-privacy law (consent, identification, working opt-out);
8. probe or exploit weaknesses in another user's apps or data you reach through the Services.

## 3. Platform integrity {#integrity}

You must not:

1. create multiple or fake accounts to evade a ban, limit, quota, trial, price, referral or promotion rule, or to manipulate rankings, votes, ratings or metrics;
2. share, sell, rent or transfer accounts, seats, API keys or licences;
3. resell or provide access to the Services to third parties except as your plan expressly allows;
4. scrape, crawl, spider, harvest or bulk-download data from the Services, or build derived databases, except through the documented API within its limits and terms;
5. reverse engineer or extract source code, models, prompts, weights or non-public interfaces, or use the Services or their output to build, train or improve a competing product or model unless we agree in writing;
6. misrepresent your identity, affiliation or authority, or use our name, brand or logos in a way that implies endorsement;
7. abuse billing, refund, chargeback, credit or support processes, including knowingly false claims of non-delivery or unauthorised use;
8. use the Services to circumvent the rules of a third-party platform or someone else's terms.{{#if feature.game}}
9. cheat, use bots or unauthorised software, exploit bugs, trade accounts or virtual items for real money, boost, or manipulate results, rankings or matchmaking in games. The detailed rules, anti-cheat notice and penalties are in the [Game Rules & Virtual Items Policy]({{url.game_rules}}).{{/if}}

## 5. AI misuse {#ai}

{{#if feature.ai}}
In addition to the rules above, you must not use AI features to:

1. generate malware, phishing, scams, targeted harassment, weapons-development or other seriously harmful instructions;
2. produce content for non-consensual intimate imagery, impersonation, or fake evidence;
3. make fully automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on people (employment, credit, housing, insurance, education, public benefits, law enforcement, immigration, healthcare) without meaningful human review and where the law allows;
4. perform emotion recognition at work or school, biometric categorisation by sensitive traits, social scoring, untargeted facial-image scraping, or other practices prohibited under applicable AI regulation (including the EU AI Act);
5. attempt prompt injection, jailbreaking, system-prompt extraction, model extraction, or to defeat safety systems, or train another model on outputs where prohibited in our documentation;
6. present AI output as professional advice (legal, medical, financial) or as human-created where that would deceive.

Unless we have agreed in writing, do not use the Services for: emergency services; medical diagnosis or treatment; critical infrastructure or industrial safety; vehicle, aviation or weapons control; or payment-card, health or other regulated data that our infrastructure is not designed to hold. ## 7. Fair use of free and promotional plans {#fair-use}

Free tiers, trials and promotions are intended for genuine evaluation and personal or small-scale use. Using them to run production workloads at scale, to mine resources, to resell, or by repeatedly signing up anew is abuse. We may apply reasonable limits and may require an upgrade, without notice where abuse is automated or evident.

We may investigate suspected violations and, **at our discretion and in proportion to the seriousness, frequency, intent and harm**, take any of these actions, alone or together, with or without prior notice where the law permits:

1. warn you or ask you to fix the issue;
2. remove, disable, label or demote content or restrict its visibility;
3. throttle, limit or block features, API keys, IP addresses or traffic;
4. suspend the account temporarily or limit its functions;
5. terminate the account and ban its owner from re-registering (see Terms section 17);
6. withhold or set off payments and refuse refunds for periods of violation;
7. recover our costs and seek injunctive and other legal relief (Terms section 21);
8. notify affected users, platforms, payment providers, and **report to law enforcement or regulators**, and preserve and disclose records as the law allows or requires.

Serious or illegal violations (such as child exploitation, malware, fraud, imminent harm, attacks on our systems) lead to immediate termination. We are not liable for loss from enforcement taken in good faith under this policy. We may also act on court orders, authority requests and reports from rights holders.

**Your right to know and appeal.** Where required by law (for example the EU Digital Services Act), when we remove content or restrict or terminate your account we tell you the facts and grounds, whether automated tools were used, and how to contest the decision. You can ask for human review of any enforcement decision by writing to [{{company.legal_email}}](mailto:{{company.legal_email}}) within **6 months**; we respond promptly and reverse the action if we got it wrong. Abusive, bad-faith or repeated unfounded appeals and reports may be ignored.

Report violations, illegal content, security problems or suspected account compromise to [{{company.legal_email}}](mailto:{{company.legal_email}}).
