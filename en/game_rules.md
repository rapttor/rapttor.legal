# Game Rules & Virtual Items Policy

*Version {{doc.version}}, last updated {{doc.updated}}.*

These rules supplement the [Terms of Service]({{url.terms}}) and the [Acceptable Use Policy]({{url.acceptable_use}}) for {{product.name}} and other games and interactive experiences from **{{company.name}}** (the "**Game**"). If they conflict on a game-specific point, these rules prevail.

1. **Licence, not ownership.** Virtual currency, items, skins, characters, progress and similar content ("**Virtual Items**") are licensed to you on a limited, personal, revocable, non-transferable, non-sublicensable basis for use in the Game only. You do not own them and have no property right in them, your account or its progress.
2. **No cash value.** Virtual Items are not money, e-money, securities or stored value; they cannot be redeemed for cash or goods, except where mandatory law says otherwise.
3. **No trading outside the Game.** You must not sell, buy, gift, trade, lend, auction or otherwise transfer Virtual Items, accounts or progress for real money or anything of value outside features we provide. Such **real-money trading** and any third-party marketplace use is prohibited; items and accounts involved may be removed and the accounts banned. We are not responsible for losses from dealing with third parties.
4. **Prices and clarity.** We show prices of Virtual Items in real currency (or the price of the currency pack and what it buys) before you confirm a purchase, and do not make progress impossible without paying where the law forbids it. Taxes are included for consumers where required.
5. **Spending controls.** We provide, or the platform provides, tools to limit or block purchases (device or store parental controls and any in-Game spending limit). Purchases made on a device or account by anyone with access are your responsibility; {{#if feature.payments}}see [Refunds]({{url.refunds}}) for how to ask us to review an unauthorised or accidental purchase by a minor.{{else}}contact us to review an unauthorised or accidental purchase by a minor.{{/if}}
6. **Changes.** We may change, rebalance, rename, withdraw or limit Virtual Items and features, and set expiry dates for promotional items, as the Game evolves. We will not remove paid Virtual Items you have already bought other than as stated in this policy (bans, end of service) or as the law allows.
7. **Free and earned items.** Items earned by play or given free have no value, can be reset, and are lost on account closure.

## 2. Random rewards ("loot boxes") {#random-rewards}

If the Game offers items obtained by chance (loot boxes, gacha, card packs, spins):
- we **disclose the probabilities** of each outcome category before purchase, in the Game and where required by the store;
- items obtained by chance can usually also be obtained without paying where the Game says so, and paid random rewards are **not offered where the law prohibits or restricts them**; we may disable them in those countries or for under-age players;
- random rewards do **not** give you any right to win money or anything outside the Game.

## 3. Fair play {#fair-play}

Play fairly. You must not:

1. **cheat or use unauthorised software:** hacks, aimbots, wallhacks, macros or scripts that automate play, speed hacks, memory editors, packet manipulation, modified or emulated clients, or tools that give an unfair advantage;
2. **exploit bugs or glitches** for advantage, duplicate items or currency, or bypass payment, and you must report discovered exploits to [{{company.email}}](mailto:{{company.email}}) instead of using or sharing them;
3. **bot or farm** with automated accounts, or run multiple accounts to gain advantage, collect rewards, or manipulate matchmaking;
4. **manipulate results:** win-trading, match-fixing, boosting, intentional losing or throwing, collusion, leaderboard or ranking manipulation, smurfing to harm lower-rated players;
5. **disrupt others:** DDoS or lag-switching opponents, stream sniping or ghosting in competitive play, griefing, intentional disconnecting, spawn camping beyond game rules, or spamming;
6. **share, sell, buy or boost accounts**, ranks, items or services (account trading and paid boosting);
7. **reverse engineer** the Game, servers, protocols, or anti-cheat, or create unauthorised servers or clients;
8. **circumvent bans** by creating new accounts, changing hardware or network identifiers, or using another person's account.

## 4. Anti-cheat and integrity measures {#anti-cheat}

To keep play fair we use anti-cheat and integrity systems that may run during the Game{{#if feature.mobile}} (on mobile, also device integrity checks){{/if}}. They may scan for unauthorised software and read limited technical data such as running process names, device and hardware identifiers, and game-state telemetry. By playing you consent to this processing as described in the [Privacy Policy]({{url.privacy}}). Where a kernel-level or similarly privileged component is required it is described in the install or launch notice, runs only while the Game runs where technically feasible, and does not read your files, communications or browsing. Detected violations may lead to automated and human-reviewed action under section 7.

## 5. Community conduct {#community}

Treat others with respect. In addition to section 1 of the [Acceptable Use Policy]({{url.acceptable_use}}), the following are not allowed in chat, voice, names, avatars, clan or guild names, user-created levels or items:

- hate, harassment, threats, sexual content involving or directed at minors, bullying, sexual harassment, doxxing, grooming, or attempts to move a minor to another platform;
- advertising, spam, scams, phishing, begging, impersonating staff or other players, offering real-money deals;
- real-world political campaigning, extremist symbols or glorification of violence; content infringing others' rights.

**Voice and chat safety.** To enforce these rules, chat text may be logged and filtered automatically, and voice chat reported to us may be reviewed from a short recording or transcript kept for the purposes of the report (generally no longer than {{policy.notice_days}} days unless needed for a pending case). You can mute and block players and report them in the Game.

## 7. Enforcement {#enforcement}

Proportionate to the severity, repetition and harm, we may: warn; mute or limit chat; remove content or names; reset or remove ranks, scores, progress and Virtual Items obtained unfairly; suspend or ban accounts (including by device or hardware identifier); withhold rewards; and report illegal activity to the authorities. **Virtual Items and currency on accounts closed for breach (cheating, real-money trading, fraud, chargeback abuse) are forfeited without refund** where the law allows.

We tell you the reason where practicable and the law requires it, and you may ask for review by writing to [{{company.legal_email}}](mailto:{{company.legal_email}}) within 6 months. We keep ban records to prevent repeat offences. Automated detection is reviewed by a person on appeal.

## 8. Events, tournaments and prizes {#events}

Events, tournaments, leaderboards and giveaways may have **official rules** published with the event, which prevail for it. Unless stated, there are no cash prizes; where prizes are offered they depend on skill and not on chance or purchase, are open only where lawful and to eligible ages, may require identity and residence checks, and are subject to tax on the winner. Purchases never improve your chances of winning a prize draw. We are not a gambling operator and the Game must not be used for gambling.

## 9. Availability and end of service {#end-of-service}

Online games depend on servers. We may perform maintenance, change or end features, content, modes or the Game itself. If we decide to **end the Game or a paid service**, we will give at least **60 days' notice** (shorter only where the law or an emergency requires) by email, in the Game and on our website, will stop selling Virtual Items at least 30 days before, and will say what happens to your content and, where the law requires or we offer it, how remaining paid balances are refunded or compensated. Where reasonably feasible, we will keep offline or single-player modes working or release patches or tools that allow continued play. Nothing here limits mandatory consumer rights.

## 10. Player health and safety {#health}

Playing for long periods can affect you. Take breaks. Some games contain flashing lights that may trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy; stop and consult a doctor if you feel unwell. Parents and guardians should review age ratings, parental controls and spending limits, and talk to children about safe online play.
